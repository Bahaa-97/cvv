FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CVFlow.

Is CVFlow really 100% free?
Yes! We believe everyone deserves a professional resume. There are no paywalls, no premium templates, and no watermarks on your PDF.
Are my personal details safe?
Absolutely. Your data never leaves your browser. We don't have servers, we don't save your CV, and we don't track your personal info. It's completely private.
How do I use the site?
Just pick a template you like from the Templates page, click 'Edit', fill in your details on the left, see the live preview on the right, and hit 'Download PDF' when you're done!
Do the templates pass ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)?
Yes. We offer a dedicated 'ATS Friendly' category. All our modern and ATS templates are designed to be easily readable by recruitment software.
